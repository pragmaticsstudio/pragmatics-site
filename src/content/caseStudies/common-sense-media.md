## Challenge

Common Sense Media is where millions of families go to find out what their kids should watch, read and play, and it came to Pragmatics at a moment of expansion. With a growing family of properties serving parents, educators and kids, it was ready to become something bigger: the place that helps families understand how AI and digital media shape children’s lives.

Growth like that is about more than designing a few new pages. It needed a design language that could carry the same trust across every audience and every property, and extra hands to help its design team build it quickly.

![Common Sense Media case study, image 1](/images/case-studies/common-sense-media/1.png)

## Outcome

We started with a shared visual language: a design system for the new Youth AI Safety Institute, built so a parent reading a risk rating, a teacher exploring AI fundamentals and a visitor taking a quick quiz all feel like they’re in the same trusted place. It’s now becoming the foundation for a full redesign of Common Sense’s properties.

![Common Sense Media case study, image 2](/images/case-studies/common-sense-media/2.png)

Around it, we’ve designed universal navigation that ties the properties together, mini-games that help parents practise spotting what AI can fake, and plenty of smaller pieces along the way, too many to keep count.

![Common Sense Media case study, image 3](/images/case-studies/common-sense-media/3.png)

The system debuted with the Youth AI Safety Institute, which launched on May 5, 2026 as an independent lab that crash-tests the AI products children use most.
