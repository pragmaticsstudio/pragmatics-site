## Project context

Ground News partnered with us to evolve core mobile patterns into a clearer, more scalable web experience.  
The mission was to help readers compare coverage across the political spectrum and reduce information silos.

## What we changed

- Simplified key reading and navigation flows
- Introduced and iterated dozens of web layouts
- Designed companion experiences for social and browser contexts
- Established repeatable UI patterns for rapid release cycles

## Why it mattered

The team moved quickly from concept to shipped iterations, with design integrated into technical and marketing workflows.  
By focusing on context, clarity, and framing, the product better supported balanced media consumption at scale.
