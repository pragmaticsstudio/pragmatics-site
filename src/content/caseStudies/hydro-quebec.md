Quebec’s Digital Technology Group during 2023 to introduce a design system and operations for their over 800 internal applications. The design system would enable closer collaboration between the functional analysts and the developers. The goal was to speed up software innovation and improve user experience of systems critical for maintaining Quebec’s electric grid.</p><p>Our work included conducting stakeholder analysis through qualitative interviews, industry benchmarking, heuristic analysis of their existing applications, and educational sessions to train staff on design operations.</p><p>We are very proud to have been a part of initiating this digital transformation.
