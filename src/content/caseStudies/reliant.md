class="project-content-grid-left-inner"><h3 class="heading-h3 margin-bottom margin-xsmall w-dyn-bind-empty"></h3><div class="case-study-text-rich-text w-richtext"><h3>Introduction</h3><p>In an era where information overload complicates decision-making, Reliant aims to simplify the process. By harnessing the power of generative AI, Reliant&#x27;s advanced machine learning software sifts through vast amounts of data to deliver precise, comprehensive answers to even the most complex queries. Reliant approached us with a unique challenge: to craft a compelling brand identity that encapsulates their innovative approach to AI and machine learning.</p><h3>Finding knots in a web of information</h3><p>We set out to develop a brand identity for Reliant that reflects their commitment to clarity, precision, and innovation. The knot emerged as a shape that spoke to the idea of complications. By imagining a web of data, the knot became a way to for Reliant to signal it’s ability to help scientists discover insight and unbundle them. We designed a logo that symbolizes the a knotted rope in the shape of an R. It’s meant to representing Reliant’s ability to distill complex information and untie them into clear, actionable insights.</p><h3>Grounded innovation</h3><p>The visual identity for Reliant centres around the concept of clarity and insight. The colour palette combines earthy tones that clashes with the typical electric colours of AI companies. This helped ground it to convey both reliability and while selected neon accent colours demonstrate innovation.</p><p>To complement the visual identity, we selected a modern, clean typeface that enhances readability—a critical element for parsing data.</p><h3>Lightweight branding for heavyweight startups</h3><p>As an emerging player in the space, we had to be mindful of how much branding is necessary for Reliant’s needs. We created a brandbook that has informed several design elements by other designers and marketers. This includes brochures, presentations, and motion graphics. Each piece was informed by our branding work and reinforces the brand’s key messages and visual identity.</p><p>By developing a light but strong brand identity for Reliant, we helped them position themselves as innovators in the AI and machine learning space. The new branding not only reflects Reliant’s technological prowess but also ensures that their innovative solutions are accessible and appealing to an audience careful about their scientific integrity.</p><p>We are excited to see how Reliant’s new brand identity will support their growth and success in the coming years.</p><p>‍</p></div><div class="margin-bottom margin-large"></div><h3 class="heading-h3 margin-bottom margin-xsmall w-dyn-bind-empty"></h3><div class="case-study-text-rich-text w-dyn-bind-empty w-richtext"></div><div class="margin-bottom margin-small"></div><h3 class="heading-h3 margin-bottom margin-xsmall w-dyn-bind-empty"></h3><div class="case-study-text-rich-text w-dyn-bind-empty w-richtext"></div></div></div><div class="line-vertical"></div><div id="w-node-_75509075-c9dc-2656-885f-07c06759690a-392211f2" class="project-grid-right"><div class="services-image-wrapper w-condition-invisible"><div class="code-embed video w-condition-invisible w-embed"><div style="width: 100%; height: 100%;" class="w-background-video w-background-video-atom">
