<p class="clean-label">Experimentation Lab</p>
<h2>There’s a little mad science in our methods.</h2>
<p>Experimentation is ingrained throughout our philosophy and process. We know a lot about design, and that includes knowing that you can never know everything. That’s why we make room for no-holds-barred experimentation to surprise ourselves and discover brand-new things we didn’t know we didn’t know. In our Experimentation Lab, we let ideas take their own shape without expectations. The only goal is to try new things simply for the joy of trying. We’re proactively open to discovering better ways of doing everything. Experiments lead to collaboration, break down silos, and, when matched with transparency, are a magic formula for innovation.</p>
</div>

<section class="clean-block" id="values">
<p class="clean-label">Our Values</p>
<h2>We get to do what we love for a living. Through our values, we share that joy with as many people as we can.</h2>
<div class="clean-values">
<div class="clean-sub clean-sub--tight">
<h3>Curiosity</h3>
<p>With beginners' minds, we believe that asking good questions is the first key to sparking ideas. We thrive on big, scary challenges, going deep to learn processes, systems, and details, then finding the order that can emerge from all that glorious chaos. We’re comfortable in discomfort and getting to know the unknown.</p>
</div>
<div class="clean-sub clean-sub--tight">
<h3>Transparency</h3>
<p>We believe in radical transparency — not just visibility into our process but as an engine for idea generation. We know there is great potential in openly sharing and the empowerment for us all that comes as a result.</p>
<p>We help clients to truly understand, not just execute, through extra educational assets like video tutorials and step-by-step walkthroughs. For our students and community, we use transparency and empathy to foster engagement, trust, risk-taking, and psychological safety.</p>
</div>
<div class="clean-sub clean-sub--tight">
<h3>Good Times</h3>
<p>Let them roll… Our work is serious, but we don’t take ourselves too seriously. Life is too short to not have fun and enjoy coming to work. We love what we do and spend a lot of time doing it, and we don’t tamper that down or try to be anything other than ourselves. Authenticity is key to connection, and you’ll get to know us — the real, authentic us — every step of the way.</p>
</div>
<div class="clean-sub clean-sub--tight">
<h3>Work Comes First</h3>
<p>Above all else, we want to create meaningful work that matters. With a balance between dignity and humility, everything we do and say is in service of your project and its goals. We challenge constructively to serve the work — not our biases or personal opinions. We believe you can have expertise without an ego to match it.</p>
<p>Design is in the details, and though we never micromanage to slow work down, we get into the nitty gritty in service of the project whenever it’s needed. No aspect of the process is ever too small for our care and attention.</p>
</div>
</div>
</section>
