<summary>How do you ensure AI features are user-friendly and meet real user needs?</summary>
<p>We focus on human-centered AI design, conducting in-depth user research to understand real user needs. Our process includes iterative testing to ensure AI interactions are intuitive and aligned with user behaviors.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>How do you help mitigate the risks of AI bias and inaccuracies?</summary>
<p>While we don't control AI algorithms, we help clients by educating them on how to mitigate bias and inaccuracies. This includes guiding them toward ethical AI practices and ensuring the AI is only being used in places that users feel are safe and relevant.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>How do you approach integrating AI into existing systems?</summary>
<p>We collaborate closely with your team to understand your current workflows and infrastructure. Our goal is to ensure that AI integrates without disrupting existing processes, while optimizing efficiency.</p>
</details>
<details>
