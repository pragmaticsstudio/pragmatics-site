HEALTH CASE STUDY

Empowering Patients with a Digital Health Coach

Scenario

ColloghCares sought to help underserved Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) patients manage their condition using a digital coach paired with fitness trackers and health monitors. They needed a patient-friendly app that improved adherence to CKD management programs, especially for vulnerable populations who struggle with affordable healthcare.

Outcome

Pragmatics designed an intuitive, patient-centered app, combining medical best practices with innovative UX. The app is now entering FDA approval and testing, set to boost medical adherence and reduce hospital visits, empowering patients to manage CKD at home.

Why Pragmatics? The Benefits of Great UX in Health & MedTech

Our approach to designing healthcare solutions starts by considering what would empower patients, ease healthcare providers’ lives, and improve outcomes. With our human-centered approach, we ensure that your digital health products not only meet compliance standards but also deliver experiences that enhance patient care.
  • Boost Patient Adherence: Intuitive interfaces and personalized nudges help patients stick to their treatment plans and improve their health outcomes.
  • Streamline Workflows & Reduce Errors: Carefully considered designs reduce cognitive load for healthcare professionals, helping them navigate complex systems quickly and efficiently. This enhances productivity, lowers the risk of errors, and improves patient outcomes

FAQ

Common Questions

How does UX design improve patient outcomes in healthcare?

Good UX design ensures that digital health tools are intuitive and easy to use, leading to higher patient engagement, more accurate data input, and quicker access to critical health information. This supports better decision-making by healthcare providers and encourages patients to take an active role in managing their health.

How do you ensure compliance with healthcare regulations (e.g., HIPAA, FDA)?

We collaborate closely with legal experts and medical professionals to ensure that all designs meet stringent healthcare regulations like HIPAA and FDA requirements. Our process integrates compliance from the ground up to create secure, compliant digital solutions.

Will integrating new UX designs disrupt our existing systems?

We aim to minimize disruptions by working closely with your team to ensure seamless integration of new designs with your existing workflows. Our phased implementation approach ensures that all stakeholders are comfortable with the changes while maintaining continuity of care.

How long does a typical UX design project take in the healthcare space?

Timelines vary based on the project's scope, but a typical UX project can take 8 to 16 weeks from research to final design. We provide a clear roadmap from the outset, with regular check-ins and feedback loops to keep the project on track.
