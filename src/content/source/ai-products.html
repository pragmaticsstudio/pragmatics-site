Case Study

Building More Cohesive Online Communities

Scenario

Waverly, a new social media platform co-founded by Philippe Beaudoin, sought to disrupt traditional social networks by empowering users to customize their community algorithms for deeper, more empathetic conversations. How We Build User-Focused AI Experiences

At Pragmatics, we take a rigorous, user informed approach to UX in AI. Our focus is on designing AI systems that are intuitive, effective, and thoroughly tested to ensure they truly meet user needs.
  • Respecting User Context: We base AI design on real user needs and contextual research, ensuring the AI adapts to the way people actually work and interact, not just theoretical scenarios.
  • Iterative Prototyping: We prototype and refine AI interactions through continuous testing cycles, ensuring the AI functions smoothly and adapts to real-world use cases.
  • Collaborative Integration: We work closely with your entire team and stakeholders, providing education and insights on AI to ensure everyone understands the technology and its capabilities, fostering informed decision-making and smoother adoption.

FAQ

Common Questions

How do you ensure AI features are user-friendly and meet real user needs?

We focus on human-centered AI design, conducting in-depth user research to understand real user needs. Our process includes iterative testing to ensure AI interactions are intuitive and aligned with user behaviors.

How do you help mitigate the risks of AI bias and inaccuracies?

While we don't control AI algorithms, we help clients by educating them on how to mitigate bias and inaccuracies. This includes guiding them toward ethical AI practices and ensuring the AI is only being used in places that users feel are safe and relevant.

How do you approach integrating AI into existing systems?

We collaborate closely with your team to understand your current workflows and infrastructure. Our goal is to ensure that AI integrates without disrupting existing processes, while optimizing efficiency.

How do you ensure stakeholders understand the AI system and its impact?

We actively involve stakeholders throughout the design process. We offer workshops, onboarding sessions, and transparent documentation to educate them on how AI works and how it benefits their product.
